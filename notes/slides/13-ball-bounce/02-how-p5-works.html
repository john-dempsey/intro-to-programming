How p5 works

  • width/height as the boundary source of truth. Sketch 12 used p5's width/height system variables (set once by createCanvas() back in sketch 01) inside a wrap-around calculation. Here they're used the same way, as ordinary numbers, but for a different purpose — as the coordinates of the canvas's far edges, to compare ball positions against.
