How p5 works

  • Moving the loop from setup() into draw() is what turns a static picture into an animation. Sketch 10 called .display() on every ball inside setup() — once. Here, the identical-looking loop is inside draw() instead, and p5 calls draw() ~60 times a second (the render loop, from sketch 02). Each call, every ball moves by one small step (.move()) and is redrawn at its new position (.display()) — individually tiny changes that, repeated 60 times a second, accumulate into visible motion. This is the render loop mechanic from sketch 02, now combined with the objects-in-an-array mechanic from sketches 09–10, for the first time.
