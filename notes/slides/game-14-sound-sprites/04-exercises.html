Exercises

Working copy: exercises/game-14-sound-sprites/. Full instructions are in that folder's README.md. Run it with Live Server, and press M in the game to turn sound on and off.

  1. Predict, change, observe. Where do the colours come from? What does noSmooth() change?
  2. Load the page and listen. Why is there no sound until you press a key?
  3. Missing files. Open the page without a server; rename a file; read the Network tab.
  4. Break it. Remove the failure callback, or the check in playSound(), and see what a missing file does then.
  5. Debugger and console. Look inside sprites and sounds; see what order the files arrive in.
  6. Write a test. Use a fake sound to test that hitting an alien plays the explosion.
  7. Bug hunt. Some aliens look wrong but every test passes: read the console and the Network tab.
  8. Git. A .gitignore file for things that shouldn't be committed.
  9. Your project. What happens if one of your files is missing? Is every sound played through one function?
