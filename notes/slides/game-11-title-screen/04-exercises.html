Exercises

Working copy: exercises/game-11-title-screen/. Full instructions are in that folder's README.md.

  1. Predict, change, observe. Does the high score survive a reload, a browser restart, a private window?
  2. DevTools. Look inside localStorage; edit and delete the saved value.
  3. Break it. Make saving fail on purpose, with and without try/catch.
  4. Debugger. Follow the state changes with a logpoint; check endGame() runs once.
  5. Write a test. The end-of-game message; a negative saved score; how to make endGame() testable.
  6. Bug hunt. The title screen gets skipped: two ifs or else if?
  7. Git. See the history as a graph; go back and play an old version.
  8. Challenge. Find a hidden bug with git bisect, in pairs.
  9. Your project. A state diagram, saving with try/catch, and a progress update to your plan.
