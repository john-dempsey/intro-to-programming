How I developed this

Attempt 1: dist()

The ball-against-ball exercise in sketch 13 used dist(), so I started with that: a hit if the bullet's centre was within 15 pixels of the alien's centre.

It mostly worked, but some shots felt wrong. Shots that clipped the corner of an alien went straight through it, and bullets coming up from below seemed to vanish just before they reached the alien. To see what was going on, I added the hitbox drawing to the debug overlay, drawing a circle of radius 15 around each alien.

The picture explained everything. The aliens are 30 × 20 rectangles: the circle cut off their corners and stuck out above and below them. A circle is the wrong shape for a rectangle. I switched to a rectangle overlap test, and the hitboxes now match exactly what's drawn.

Decision: getBounds()

The overlap test needs four numbers for each rectangle. My first version of boundsOverlap() took eight parameters, and I got them in the wrong order twice in the first ten minutes. Giving every class a getBounds() method that returns a { left, right, top, bottom } object meant the function only needs two parameters, and it can't mix them up. It also made the tests much easier to write, with hand-made rectangles.

Thinking about break before writing the loop

I'd been caught by removing items inside a loop at stage 4, so this time I traced it on paper before running anything: one bullet, and a column of aliens it's touching. Without break, after removing the bullet, the inner loop would keep checking it against more aliens. In the game, aliens never overlap, so a bullet can't touch two at once and I'd never see the bug. I added break anyway, and a test with three aliens stacked on top of each other to prove it works. That's a case that can't happen in the game today, but could if aliens ever got closer together.

Manual checks

  • A bullet that hits an alien removes both, straight away.
  • A bullet that passes between two aliens hits neither.
  • Clipping the very corner of an alien counts as a hit.
  • With hitboxes on (D), the boxes fit the shapes exactly.
  • Destroying the right-hand column lets the formation travel further right.
  • Shooting every alien leaves an empty screen (nothing happens yet: that's stage 8).
  • The console shows only PASS lines.
