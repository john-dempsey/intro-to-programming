Game 07 — Collisions

Exercises

Working copy: exercises/game-07-collisions/. Full instructions are in that folder's README.md. Press D to see the hitboxes.

  1. Predict, change, observe. Destroy the right-hand column; wide bullets; aliens wide enough to overlap.
  2. The console. Call boundsOverlap() and getBounds() directly, and check the answers on squared paper.
  3. Break it. Remove break (a bug you can't see by playing); decide whether touching counts as a hit.
  4. Debugger. Pause on a hit and work through the overlap test by hand.
  5. Write a test. Corner overlaps, the ship's bounds, and two bullets aimed at one alien.
  6. Bug hunt. One bullet destroys the wrong alien.
  7. Git. Fix a commit message with git commit --amend.
  8. Your project. Rectangles or circles? Add getBounds() and draw the hitbox.
