Game 10 — The Aliens Fire Back

Exercises

Working copy: exercises/game-10-aliens-fire/. Full instructions are in that folder's README.md.

  1. Predict, change, observe. Predict the number of shots in 10 seconds; try different fire rates and speeds.
  2. Randomness. Use randomSeed() to make every game the same.
  3. The console. Make an alien fire on demand; set your lives.
  4. Break it. Stop removing bullets at the bottom; lose every life to one bullet.
  5. Debugger. Find the bullet that hit you; pause only on your last life.
  6. Write a test. Bullets below the canvas, touching the ship, and testing something random.
  7. Bug hunt. Game over before you've started. Why does the bug look different without the tests?
  8. Git. Create and resolve a merge conflict.
  9. Challenge. Only the lowest alien in each column can fire.
  10. Your project. randomSeed() for debugging; events that should happen once.
