Game 10 — The Aliens Fire Back

▶ Run this sketch Sketch: src/game/10-aliens-fire/sketch.js · Tests: src/game/10-aliens-fire/tests.js

The aliens fire back. Each frame, there's a small chance that a random alien fires a bullet downwards. The player has three lives; being hit loses one.

New ideas: randomness, and why it makes testing and debugging harder; reusing one class for two jobs by giving bullets a velocity, as the balls had in sketch 11; and a hit that must count only once, even though the bullet touches the ship for several frames. The exercises cover randomSeed() and resolving a merge conflict.
