Exercises

Working copy: exercises/game-00-plan/. Full instructions are in that folder's README.md.

These exercises are about planning, not code. Exercises 3–5 are a start on planning your own project.

  1. Spot the features. Play Space Invaders online and list features missing from the plan. Sort each into must, should or could have.
  2. Break a feature down. Split "aliens march, drop and reverse" into at least four smaller steps you could each run and see.
  3. Your project: one sentence. Describe your project in one sentence.
  4. Your project: the plan. Fill in PLAN-TEMPLATE.md for your own project.
  5. Your project: questions. List three things you don't know how to do yet, and where you'd look first.
  6. Git: read the history. Use git log --oneline and rewrite one of your own unclear commit messages.
