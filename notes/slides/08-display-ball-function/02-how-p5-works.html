How p5 works

Worth contrasting explicitly: displayBall() is a function students wrote and call themselves, whenever they choose (here, inside mousePressed()) — unlike setup(), draw() and mousePressed(), which only p5 calls, and only at the times p5 decides. displayBall() happens to call p5's own drawing functions (fill(), circle()) inside it, but p5 has no idea displayBall() exists.
