Exercises

Working copy: exercises/game-15-ufo/, the finished game. Full instructions are in that folder's README.md. Run it with Live Server, and read the final PLAN.md and CHANGELOG.md before you start.

  1. Predict, change, observe. How often does a UFO come? Change the waits and the speed.
  2. The console. Control time: summon a UFO, check its points, make it vanish.
  3. Break it. Pass the wrong time; swap two lines in handleUfoHit() and read the error.
  4. Debugger. Pause as a UFO is created, and find out which side it will come from before it appears.
  5. Write a test. How many frames does a UFO take to reach the middle? Why do the schedule tests check a range?
  6. Bug hunt. UFOs come far too often: follow what happens after one leaves.
  7. Git. Release version 1.0 with an annotated tag, and write a changelog entry.
  8. Retrospective. Look back over the game stages you've worked through.
  9. Your project. A progress section, a retrospective, a changelog, and a v1.0 tag when it's finished.
