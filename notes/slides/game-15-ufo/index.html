Game 15 — The Mystery UFO

▶︎ Run this sketch Sketch: src/game/15-ufo/sketch.js · Tests: src/game/15-ufo/tests.js · Plan: PLAN.md · Changelog: CHANGELOG.md

Every 15 to 25 seconds, a UFO flies across the top of the screen, from a random side. Shooting it scores 50, 100, 150 or 300 points, and the player never knows which.

That's the last feature in the plan, so this stage also finishes the project: the plan gets a "Retrospective" looking back at what went well and what to do differently, a CHANGELOG.md lists every version for players, and the finished game is released as version 1.0.

New ideas: measuring time in milliseconds rather than frames; scheduling something for a random time in the future; and passing the time into a function so that tests can choose what time it is. The exercises control time from the console, and tag the release in Git.

Run your own copy with Live Server, as in stage 14.
