Exercises

Working copy: exercises/game-08-win-lose/. Full instructions are in that folder's README.md. Play the game properly at least once first.

  1. Predict, change, observe. How long until the aliens arrive; where "too low" should be; why the ship stops after the game ends.
  2. The console. Jump straight to winning or losing, instead of playing until you get there.
  3. Break it. Fire after the game ends; keep updating behind the message; compare a mistyped constant with a mistyped string.
  4. Debugger. Catch the moment the game is lost; count how often resetGame() runs with a logpoint.
  5. Write a test. An alien below the ship; decide whether reset should turn off the overlay.
  6. Bug hunt. The aliens vanish and you can never win: one missing character.
  7. Git. Tag the smallest playable game as a milestone.
  8. Challenge. Add a paused state.
  9. Your project. Your project's states, a reset function, and a milestone tag.
