Game 06 — The Alien March

▶ Run this sketch Sketch: src/game/06-alien-march/sketch.js · Tests: src/game/06-alien-march/tests.js

The aliens march. The whole formation moves sideways together. When any alien is about to go past an edge, every alien drops down and the formation reverses. The aliens keep marching off the bottom of the screen for now; winning and losing come at stage 8.

This looks like sketch 13's bounce, and that's exactly the trap: the balls in sketch 13 each bounced on their own, but the aliens must act as a group. The "How I developed this" page describes two bugs that came from reusing the bounce. The exercises introduce logpoints and git revert.
